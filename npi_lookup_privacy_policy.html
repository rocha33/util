1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how NPI Lookup ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application (the "App") available on Google Play.

We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency. NPI Lookup is a lightweight utility tool designed for healthcare professionals, medical billers, administrative staff, and patients. We do not require user account registration, we do not require logins, and we do not operate a proprietary backend server to store your personal details.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect or request:

  • Your real name, personal email address, or phone number.
  • Passwords or user account credentials.
  • Financial, credit card, or payment data.
  • Precise GPS location data.
  • Device contacts, photos, microphone, or media files.

3. Information Handled by the App

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is saved exclusively in your device's sandboxed local application storage:

  • Recent Searches: A local history of your latest provider or NPI searches to help you quickly re-run queries.
  • Favorite Providers: NPI records you choose to bookmark (star) for quick offline access.
  • Temporary Cache: Cached public registry responses to accelerate performance and minimize repeat network calls.

We never transmit, back up, or store this local data on external servers. You can completely delete this local data at any time by:

  1. Opening the App → About → tapping Clear all data, or
  2. Clearing the App's storage via Android Settings → Apps → NPI Lookup → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage.

B. Information Transmitted Over the Network

When you perform an NPI or provider search, the App sends your query parameters (such as the 10-digit NPI number, provider name, medical specialty/taxonomy, city, or state) via an encrypted HTTPS connection directly to the official CMS NPPES public API endpoint (https://npiregistry.cms.hhs.gov/api/).

This network request does not include your personal identity, device contacts, or Google Account information.

C. Outbound Links to Third-Party Services

The App contains convenient deep links to external resources, such as:

  • Official State Medical Board license verification pages (e.g., Florida DOH, California DCA, Texas Medical Board, New York State Education Department).
  • U.S. Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA) public forms.
  • CMS Provider Enrollment, Chain, and Ownership System (PECOS).
  • Native phone dialer ("Call"), mapping apps ("Map"), or email clients ("Share").

When you tap on these links, your device opens your external web browser or corresponding native app. Any information you provide to or receive from those third parties is governed solely by their respective privacy policies and terms of service.

7. Data Security

Although the App does not store personal data on remote servers, we prioritize user security:

  • All communications with the public NPPES registry API occur exclusively over encrypted HTTPS (TLS).
  • Locally saved search records and bookmarks are restricted to the application's secure private sandbox on your Android device.

Depending on your geographic location, you may have specific privacy rights regarding personal data:
