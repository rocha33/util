Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026 · App: Acervo: AI Photo Search (acervo.vr.documentos.gallery.ai) · Developer: EitaVicio

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android app Acervo: AI Photo Search (package acervo.vr.documentos.gallery.ai), published on Google Play by the developer EitaVicio ("we", "us").

Acervo is an Android app for offline semantic search of your photos and videos, smart cleanup, documents and receipts, and smart albums. This policy explains what the app accesses, what it does with it, and your choices.

1. Summary

  • Your photos, videos, documents and the index built from them stay on your device. We do not upload them, and we have no server that receives them.
  • Analysis (search, OCR, similarity) runs on the device.
  • The app connects to the internet to download the AI model once, to show ads (Google AdMob), and for Google Play update and review features.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

2. Data the app accesses on your device

  • Photos and videos (file names, folders, dates, sizes, thumbnails and, when permitted, location metadata) to build the local search index, find similar photos and screenshots, and build albums.
  • Documents (PDF, Word and Excel files, up to 30 MB each) and photos of receipts, to list them and read their text.
  • Text recognized in images (merchant, date, amount) extracted on the device with Google ML Kit text recognition (bundled in the app) and saved only locally.
  • App settings (language, albums you create, warranty reminders, biometric lock on/off) stored locally.

This information is not sent to us or to third parties. App data is excluded from Android backup and device transfer.

3. Permissions

PermissionWhy the app needs it

4. The AI model download

On first run the app downloads the EmbeddingGemma model (about 462 MB) from Hugging Face (huggingface.co/valdec/embeddinggemma-2-740m-litert-lm). The download request exposes your IP address and basic device/connection data to that host under its own privacy policy. The file is verified (size and SHA-256) before use. No user content is sent in this process.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads and, depending on your consent, to personalize them. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), the app shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) and you can change your choice later. You can also reset or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads). Learn more: policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Google Play services

The app uses Google Play in-app update and in-app review. These are provided by Google Play and subject to Google's privacy policy. The app does not receive your Google account data through them.

7. Deleting files

The cleanup feature only deletes files after you review and confirm. On Android 11+ files go to the system trash; on Android 10 and below, deletion is permanent and the app warns you first. Favorites are never selected automatically.

8. Data retention and your control

Local data stays until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Removing an item from an album or the document list does not delete the original file. You can revoke permissions, turn off the biometric lock and reset ad consent at any time.

9. Children

Acervo is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

10. Security

Because data is processed and stored on your device, its protection depends on your device's security. The model download uses HTTPS and integrity verification.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest revision; material changes will be reflected in the app listing or this page.

12. Contact
