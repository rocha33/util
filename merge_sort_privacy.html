Merge Sort

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This policy explains how the Android app Merge Sort: Color Puzzle (package com.mergesortgame.merge_sort_game) handles information. The game runs on your device. There is no player account with email or password.

The app shows ads through Google AdMob and may use the Android Advertising ID. See section 5.

1. Who is responsible

  • App: Merge Sort: Color Puzzle
  • Android package: com.mergesortgame.merge_sort_game
  • Publisher: VR Games
  • Privacy contact: vrgames.app@gmail.com

This notice is meant to comply with Brazil’s LGPD and, where applicable, the EU/UK GDPR. Google Play also requires a public privacy policy for apps that use ads or the Advertising ID.

2. Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or a traditional sign-up.
  • Game progress (scores, campaign stars, mute) stays on the device (app storage and WebView local storage).
  • We show Google ads. Google may collect device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads.
  • Google Play may be used for in-app review prompts and update checks.
  • Play Games sign-in and leaderboards are optional and only run if that feature is turned on and you sign in.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

3. Information we process

Data Where it lives Why
Game progress (classic score, campaign stars, mute, settings) On the device (WebView local storage and app files) Keep your run, high score, and preferences
App-open calendar days and whether a review was requested On the device (SharedPreferences) Decide when to show Google Play’s in-app review dialog
Advertising ID, IP address, device/network data, ad events Google (AdMob / Google advertising services) Show ads, measure ads, fight fraud and invalid traffic

We do not operate our own game server for this app. Progress is not uploaded to VR Games.

4. Legal bases (LGPD / GDPR)

  • Contract / legitimate interest: storing progress on the device so the game works.
  • Legitimate interest: asking for a Play Store review after several days of use; checking for Play Store updates.
  • Consent / Google’s ad terms: personalized ads and Advertising ID, subject to your device ad settings and any consent form Google shows.

5. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app displays ads provided by Google AdMob (currently a banner; other Google ad formats may be used). Google acts as an independent controller for much of the advertising data it collects.

Ads may use:

  • The Android Advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from IP
  • Device type, language, time zone, and network information
  • Ad views, clicks, and diagnostic data

Google’s policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from ads
  • AdMob & privacy

To limit ad personalization on Android: Settings → Google → Ads → opt out of Ads Personalization (wording varies by Android version). You can also reset the Advertising ID in the same menu. This does not remove all ads; it mainly affects personalization.

6. Google Play services

  • In-app review: after a few calendar days we may ask Google Play to show a rating dialog. Play handles that flow. We only store a local flag so we do not ask again.
  • In-app updates: the app may check Google Play for a newer version when you open it.
  • Play Games: if ranking is enabled in a later version and you choose to sign in, Google processes your Play Games identity and scores under Google’s terms. Sign-in is optional. The core puzzle works without it.

7. Permissions

  • INTERNET — ads, Play review, Play updates, optional Play Games
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — detect connectivity
  • AD_ID — Google advertising identifier for AdMob

These are normal install-time permissions. The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or precise GPS.

8. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by:

  • Google LLC and Google Ireland Limited (AdMob, Play, Play Games)
  • Device backup: Android Backup may copy app data (including local progress) to your Google account if backup is on

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the app against abuse.

9. Retention and deletion

  • Local game data remains until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app.
  • Ad and Play data follow Google’s retention periods. We cannot delete Google’s advertising logs from this page.
  • To request deletion of any information VR Games might hold about you (for example email you sent us), write to vrgames.app@gmail.com.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, information about sharing, and withdrawal of consent. In Brazil you may also contact the ANPD. For ads, use the device controls in section 5 and Google’s tools.

11. Children

Merge Sort is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where that is the digital-consent age). We do not knowingly collect children’s data. Ads are not configured as a child-directed (COPPA) experience. If you believe a child provided information, email us and we will delete what we control.

12. International transfers

Google may process advertising and Play data in the United States and other countries. Those transfers follow Google’s contracts and safeguards.

13. Security

The app does not send game progress to VR Games servers. Network traffic uses HTTPS (cleartext HTTP is disabled). No method is perfect. Do not enter passwords in the game — it has no password field.

14. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top is the current version. Material changes will be posted here and, when feasible, noted in the Play Store listing.

15. Contact

Questions or LGPD/GDPR requests: vrgames.app@gmail.com.

Políticas da Google:
