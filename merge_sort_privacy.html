Merge Sort

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy explains how the Android app Merge Sort: Color Puzzle (package com.mergesortgame.merge_sort_game) handles information. The game runs on your device. There is no player account with email or password.

The app shows ads through Google AdMob and may use the Android Advertising ID. See section 5.

1. Who is responsible

  • App: Merge Sort: Color Puzzle
  • Android package: com.mergesortgame.merge_sort_game
  • Publisher: VR Games
  • Privacy contact: vrgames.app@gmail.com

This notice is meant to comply with Brazil’s LGPD and, where applicable, the EU/UK GDPR. Google Play also requires a public privacy policy for apps that use ads or the Advertising ID.

2. Summary

  • The game does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or a password. If you sign in to Play Games, Google uses that profile’s name and player ID on the leaderboard.
  • Game progress (scores, campaign stars, mute) stays on the device (app storage and WebView local storage).
  • We show Google ads: a banner and a full-screen interstitial after Classic game over. Google may collect device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads.
  • Google Play may be used for in-app review prompts and update checks.
  • Play Games sign-in is optional. If you sign in, your profile name, player ID, and score are sent to Google, and the leaderboard shows your name and score to other players. The puzzle works without signing in.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

4. Legal bases (LGPD / GDPR)

  • Contract / legitimate interest: storing progress on the device so the game works.
  • Legitimate interest: asking for a Play Store review after several days of use; checking for Play Store updates.
  • Consent / Google’s ad terms: personalized ads and Advertising ID, subject to your device ad settings and any consent form Google shows.

5. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app displays ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner, and a full-screen interstitial after Classic game over. Google acts as an independent controller for much of the advertising data it collects.

Ads may use:

  • The Android Advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from IP
  • Device type, language, time zone, and network information
  • Ad views, clicks, diagnostic data, and crash logs

Google’s policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from ads
  • AdMob & privacy

To limit ad personalization on Android: Settings → Google → Ads → opt out of Ads Personalization (wording varies by Android version). You can also reset the Advertising ID in the same menu. This does not remove all ads; it mainly affects personalization.

6. Google Play services

  • In-app review: after a few calendar days we may ask Google Play to show a rating dialog. Play handles that flow. We only store a local flag so we do not ask again.
  • In-app updates: the app may check Google Play for a newer version when you open it.
  • Play Games: sign-in is optional and available in the current app. If you sign in, Google receives your Play Games profile name, player ID, and score under Google’s terms. The leaderboard shows your name and score to other players. The puzzle works without signing in.

7. Permissions

  • INTERNET — ads, Play review, Play updates, optional Play Games
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — detect connectivity
  • AD_ID — Google advertising identifier for AdMob

These are normal install-time permissions. The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or precise GPS.

8. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by:

  • Google LLC and Google Ireland Limited (AdMob, Play, Play Games)
  • Other players, if you sign in to Play Games: your profile name and score appear on the leaderboard
  • Device backup: Android Backup may copy app data (including local progress) to your Google account if backup is on

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the app against abuse.

9. Retention and deletion

  • Local game data stays on the device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. Uninstalling deletes that local progress.
  • Advertising data and Play Games data (profile name, player ID, and scores) are held by Google and follow Google’s retention periods. VR Games cannot delete those records from this page. Use Android’s ad settings (section 5) and Play Games or Google Account tools to manage or delete them.
  • vrgames.app@gmail.com is only for messages you sent to VR Games, such as an email you wrote to us. We do not keep a player-account database.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may request access, correction, portability, information about sharing, and withdrawal of consent for information we actually hold. There is no in-app control to delete advertising or Play Games data; those requests go through Google’s tools (section 5 and Play Games settings). Email us only about messages you sent to VR Games. In Brazil you may also contact the ANPD.

11. Children

Merge Sort is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where that is the digital-consent age). We do not knowingly collect children’s data. Ads are not configured as a child-directed (COPPA) experience. If you believe a child sent us a message, email us and we will delete that message. We cannot delete Google’s advertising or Play Games records.

12. International transfers

Google may process advertising and Play data in the United States and other countries. Those transfers follow Google’s contracts and safeguards.

13. Security

The app does not send game progress to VR Games servers. Network traffic uses HTTPS (cleartext HTTP is disabled). No method is perfect. Do not enter passwords in the game — it has no password field.

14. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top is the current version. Material changes will be posted here and, when feasible, noted in the Play Store listing.

15. Contact

Questions: vrgames.app@gmail.com. Deleting advertising or Play Games data is handled by Google, not by this inbox. This address can delete messages you sent to VR Games.
